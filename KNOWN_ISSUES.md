# Known limitations (Stage 12 alpha)

This page separates **confirmed build facts** from **gameplay that has not been verified yet**.

## Confirmed
- The full Stage 12 GBA source compiled successfully on GitHub Actions.
- Source validators found 18 single-type Eeveelutions, 153 unique unordered duals, and 306 item-based single-to-dual routes.
- Land encounter tables include habitat-specific appearances for all 18 single types, plus nine rare duals.
- The type-booster item's missing evolution effect from Stage 11 was repaired in Stage 12.

## Not yet verified in a full emulator playthrough
- All early story scripts after selecting Eevee as a starter.
- Correct item consumption, level/badge gating and evolution outcomes in real party-menu use.
- Save/load/restart reliability throughout a long playthrough, especially with custom species in both party and PC.
- Every custom form's battle animation, party icon, follower orientation, Pokédex page, moves and ability.
- In-game pacing, economy, balance and wild encounter experiences.
- How the version behaves across all GBA emulators and mobile devices.

## Current limitations by design
- **Use a fresh save.** Old Evergreen or two-form prototypes have no migration guarantee.
- Only **nine** dual-type Eeveelutions appear wild; the other duals are designed to be obtained by item evolution.
- Certain custom icons/followers may look provisional even when battle artwork is present.
- The download is a **14-day GitHub Actions build artifact** in a **private** repository, not a stable public release.
- Original *Oops! All Eevees!* asset redistribution permissions are not confirmed; public distribution requires rights review and suitable packaging.
